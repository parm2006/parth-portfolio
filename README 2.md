## Featured projects

### 🧠 [SimpleEnglishRag](https://github.com/parm2006/SimpleEnglishRag)

Ask questions across Simple Wikipedia and your own files, with hybrid search and local AI doing the retrieval.

### 💾 [WinDirStat AI Companion](https://github.com/parm2006/windirstat_companion)

Explore disk usage in WinDirStat, then ask a local Ollama model to explain files and help assess cleanup risk.

### 🦀 [NeuralNetworksRust](https://github.com/parm2006/NeuralNetworksRust)

Build and train neural networks with a Rust framework featuring tensors, automatic differentiation, and optimizers.

### 🖱️ [Conduit](https://github.com/parm2006/Conduit)

Share a mouse, keyboard, clipboard, and files across Windows PCs, with low-latency video over a local network.

### 🗣️ [Orator AI](https://github.com/parm2006/Orator-AI)

Practice conversations and debates with a real-time voice AI built around speech recognition, Gemini, and voice synthesis.

### 🔎 [Scholarly Search Engine](https://github.com/parm2006/Scholarly-Search-Engine)

Search research papers from major academic sources, with citation data and author analytics to reveal work worth finding.
